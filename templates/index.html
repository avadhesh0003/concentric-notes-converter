Concentric Notes Converter — Guide & Documentation

Designed and built by Avadhesh Pingoriya, Concentric Notes Converter is an all-in-one study PDF suite made for students. Coaching and classroom digital board slides often use pitch-black or dark colored backgrounds that waste massive amounts of printer ink and make handwritten revision difficult. Concentric converts them into clean, white-background, multi-slide A4 study sheets in seconds.

How the V1.2 Smart Ink Saver Engine Works
1
Automatic Dark-Slide Detection The algorithm inspects both the outer border band and overall luminance of every slide. Only predominantly dark slides are inverted—slides that already have a white background are never turned black.
2
Local Contrast Enhancement (CLAHE) Applies Contrast Limited Adaptive Histogram Equalization (clip limit 1.25 across an 8×8 tile grid) to boost faint teacher handwriting, thin geometry lines, and low-contrast formulas before thresholding.
3
Gaussian Smoothing & V4 Adaptive Thresholding A light 3×3 Gaussian blur removes JPEG compression artifacts, followed by a 41×41 local Gaussian adaptive threshold controlled by your Outline Strength slider (default 55) to turn backgrounds pure white (#FFFFFF) and text crisp black.
4
8-Connected Component Speckle Cleanup Scans connected ink components and automatically erases microscopic isolated dust/noise specks without touching decimal points, minus signs, or diagram labels.
What Each Tool Does

1. Process Notes (Core)

Converts lecture slides using Smart Ink Saver, Invert B&W, Grayscale, or Original mode, and packs 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, or 9 slides onto each A4 page with optional borders, headers, and page numbers.

2. Merge Lectures

Combines multiple daily lecture PDFs (e.g., Lecture 01 to Lecture 08) in your custom sequence into a single complete chapter PDF.

3. Split & Extract

Pulls specific page ranges (like 1-5, 8, 12-15), odd pages, or even pages out of a large textbook or notes PDF into a new lightweight file.

4. Organize & Rotate

Lets you visually rotate sideways or upside-down pages by 90°, reorder slides left/right, or delete blank/unwanted pages.

5. Images to PDF

Turns phone photos of handwritten notebooks, whiteboards, or slide screenshots into a clean A4 PDF—with optional Smart Ink Saver filtering.

6. PDF to Images (.ZIP)

Renders selected PDF slides as high-resolution 2x PNG or JPG image files bundled neatly inside a single downloadable ZIP archive.

Important Precautions & Best Practices
1. Always Check Step 4 (1st Page Preview) First: Before converting a 60+ page lecture PDF, check the 1st A4 Page Preview in Step 4. If thin diagrams look too light, click ← Previous Step and increase Outline Strength to 65–75. If background textures appear, lower it to 35–45.
2. When to Use Grayscale or Original Instead of Smart Ink Saver: Smart Ink Saver is engineered for text, formulas, line diagrams, and dark-background slides. If a PDF contains full-page real-world photographs (like biology microscopy or geography maps), Grayscale or Original mode preserves smooth photo shading better.
3. Password-Protected PDFs: Encrypted or password-locked PDFs cannot be read by the renderer. Please remove the password before uploading.
4. Keep the Tab Open During Large Conversions: High-resolution image processing for 50–150 slides takes a few seconds in memory. Avoid closing or refreshing the browser tab while the status bar says Processing slide X of Y.
5. Printer Settings Recommendation: When printing your generated Concentric PDF on paper, set your printer scale to "Fit to Printable Area" or "100%" and select Black & White mode for the cleanest, most economical results.
